# Evidence Synthesis

- Synthesize the accepted TaskPlan, validated direct-path artifacts, accepted typed packages, and system-recorded evidence summaries from explicitly `summary_only` specialist handoffs.
- Separate direct findings, source/artifact links, data and time scope, method, validation, unresolved items, uncertainty, and limitations.
- Report specialist states, including `not invoked`, and retain the route trace.
- Carry forward a contract-determined model winner from the accepted analysis. Do not replace it with a simpler model merely because the declared metric advantage is small; report that weakness as a limitation instead.
- Do not turn association into attribution or hide alternative explanations and coverage gaps.
- The final response is an `EvidenceReport`; it is system synthesis, not an independent judge verdict.
