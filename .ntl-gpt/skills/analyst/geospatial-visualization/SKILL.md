# Geospatial Visualization

- Visualize validated analysis artifacts, not unverified intermediate guesses.
- Record CRS, extent, scale, units, NoData, classification/color limits, labels, and the source artifact checksum.
- Keep comparisons visually commensurate; do not use independent auto-stretches to manufacture apparent change.
- Ensure CJK labels render with an available font when needed and check the exported artifact.
- Maps and charts support interpretation; they do not replace numerical validation or establish causality.
